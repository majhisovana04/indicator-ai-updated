# RSI Frequently Asked Questions


## What is RSI?

RSI is a momentum indicator that measures the speed and magnitude of price movements.

---

## What does RSI above 70 mean?

An RSI above 70 generally indicates the asset may be overbought. It does not guarantee the price will fall.

---

## What does RSI below 30 mean?

It usually indicates oversold conditions and a possible upward reversal.

---

## Is RSI good for intraday trading?

Yes. Many intraday traders use RSI together with trend indicators for confirmation.

---


## Can RSI give false signals?

Yes. During strong trends, RSI may stay overbought or oversold for long periods.
