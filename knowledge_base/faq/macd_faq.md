# MACD Frequently Asked Questions

## What is MACD?

MACD is a trend-following momentum indicator that compares two moving averages to help identify potential trend changes.

---

## What does a MACD bullish crossover mean?

It means the MACD line has crossed above the signal line, which some traders interpret as a signal that upward momentum may be building.

---

## Is MACD a leading or lagging indicator?

MACD is generally considered a lagging indicator, since it's calculated from past price averages.

---

## Can MACD be used alone for trading decisions?

It's generally recommended to use MACD alongside other indicators, since it can produce false signals in sideways or choppy markets.
